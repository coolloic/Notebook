# Acme Support Knowledge Base

## Returns & Refunds
- Items may be returned within **30 days** of delivery for a full refund.
- The **customer pays return shipping** unless the item arrived damaged or incorrect.
- Refunds are issued to the original payment method within **5–7 business days** of us receiving the return.
- **Shipping fees are non-refundable** except for damaged or incorrect items.
- Digital goods are **non-refundable** once downloaded.
- Refund requests over **$500** require manual review by a specialist.

## Shipping
- Standard shipping takes **3–5 business days**; express takes **1–2 business days**.
- Free standard shipping on orders over **$50**.
- We ship to the US, Canada, the UK, and the EU.
- Tracking numbers are emailed once an order ships.

## Orders
- Orders can be **modified or cancelled within 1 hour** of being placed.
- After 1 hour, an order enters fulfillment and can no longer be changed.
- To check order status, sign in and visit **Account → Orders**.

## Accounts & Security
- Reset your password from the sign-in page via **"Forgot password"**.
- We never ask for your password by email. Report suspicious messages to security@acme.example.
- Account deletion requests are handled by a specialist and require identity verification.

## Payments
- We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, and PayPal.
- Prices are in **USD**.
- Promo codes apply to the item subtotal only, not taxes or shipping.
